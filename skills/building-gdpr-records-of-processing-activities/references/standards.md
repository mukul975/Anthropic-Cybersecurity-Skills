# Article 30 Field Reference

Source of truth: Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Article 30.
Text: https://gdpr-info.eu/art-30-gdpr/

## Article 30(1) — Controller record (mandatory fields)

| # | Field | Article 30(1) ref | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Controller name & contact | (a) | Plus joint controller, representative, DPO where they exist |
| 2 | Purposes of processing | (b) | Specific, per activity; may be multiple |
| 3 | Categories of data subjects | (c) | e.g. employees, customers, website visitors |
| 4 | Categories of personal data | (c) | Flag Article 9 (special) and Article 10 (criminal) data |
| 5 | Categories of recipients | (d) | Including recipients in third countries / international orgs |
| 6 | Third-country transfers | (e) | Name country; for Art. 49(1) 2nd-subpara transfers, document safeguards |
| 7 | Retention time limits | (f) | "Where possible" — a period or the criteria that set it |
| 8 | General Article 32 security description | (g) | "Where possible" — a general description, not full control detail |

## Article 30(2) — Processor record (mandatory fields)

| # | Field | Article 30(2) ref | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processor & each controller contact | (a) | Plus representatives and DPOs where they exist |
| 2 | Categories of processing per controller | (b) | Replaces "purposes"; what you do on their behalf |
| 3 | Third-country transfers | (c) | With Art. 49(1) 2nd-subpara safeguard documentation where applicable |
| 4 | General Article 32 security description | (d) | As for controllers |

Note the processor record does **not** require purposes, categories of data
subjects, categories of personal data, or retention periods — those remain the
controller's responsibility.

## Form and availability

- Article 30(3): the record is in writing, including electronic form.
- Article 30(4): made available to the supervisory authority on request.

## The small-organisation exemption (Article 30(5))

Does **not** apply where any of these hold — which is why most organisations
must keep a RoPA regardless of headcount:

- the processing is likely to result in a risk to data subjects' rights; **or**
- the processing is not occasional; **or**
- the processing includes special categories (Art. 9) or criminal-offence data (Art. 10).

The "fewer than 250 employees" line is only the entry condition; any one of the
above removes the exemption.

## Third-country transfer mechanisms (Chapter V)

A third-country recipient needs one of:

| Mechanism | Article | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Adequacy decision | 45 | Transfers to an adequacy-listed country |
| Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) | 46(2)(c) | 2021 EU SCCs |
| Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs) | 46(2)(b), 47 | Intra-group transfers |
| Derogations | 49 | Explicit consent, contract necessity (narrow, exceptional) |

The RoPA records *which* mechanism applies per destination; it is not the place
to reproduce the clauses themselves.
